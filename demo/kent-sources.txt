Studies have shown that changes in cigarette design have not lowered the risk of disease caused by cigarettes (US National Cancer Institute, "Light" Cigarettes and Cancer Risk).
Light cigarettes do not reduce the health risks of smoking (US National Cancer Institute).
The only guaranteed way to reduce the risk to your health, as well as the risk to others, is to stop smoking completely (US National Cancer Institute).
The principal recommendation for any smoker interested in reducing future disease risks is to quit smoking (US National Cancer Institute, Monograph 13, 2001).
